## Design Context

### Users
The primary audience is the general public: residents, partners, and visitors who will commonly browse the KKN books on mobile devices. The core jobs are discovering the available books, identifying each book from its cover and title, and reading it comfortably without leaving the site.

### Brand Personality
Clear, approachable, and credible. The interface should follow familiar shadcn/ui conventions rather than introduce a highly stylized visual identity. KKN-PPM UGM and Tanjungsari brand assets provide the visual character.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing shadcn `base-rhea` preset, Mist neutral palette, Figtree typography, Hugeicons, and existing light/dark theme support. Favor a polished publication library with restrained surfaces, responsive book covers, and a focused reader toolbar.

### Design Principles
- Make books immediately recognizable through real cover previews and plain titles.
- Prioritize mobile reading while using a two-page spread when desktop space permits.
- Keep navigation and reader controls familiar, accessible, and unobtrusive.
- Use existing shadcn components and semantic theme tokens before custom UI.
- Preserve the supplied KKN and Tanjungsari identity assets without visual distortion.
